# Sommarpris 2026 — Campaign Post-mortem

Letterhead: Nordmark Dagligvaror AB — Kronmat Category Management, Fresh & Produce
Document: Campaign review PRM Sommarpris 2026
Author: Anna Bergström, Category Manager Fresh & Produce
Reviewed by: Jonas Petersson (Kronmat controller), Johanna Lindqvist (Banner Director Kronmat)
Date: 2026-09-16

## 1. The campaign

- Banner: Kronmat. Category: Fresh & Produce. Mechanic: price cut of 30 percent on the summer fruit and vegetable range.
- Period: 2026-06-15 to 2026-08-09 (8 weeks). Participating stores: 119.
- Funding: retailer funded. Objective: traffic, in response to food price deflation and the discounters' summer campaigns.

## 2. Results

| Measure | Sommarpris 2026 | Typical Kronmat price cut |
|---|---|---|
| Sales uplift versus baseline | about 52 percent (rank 1 of 60 campaigns) | 18 to 34 percent |
| Gross margin during the campaign | about 19 percent | 24 to 26 percent |
| Margin dilution versus baseline | about 7.5 points (rank 1 from the bottom) | 2 to 5 points |
| Incremental gross profit | about SEK +5 million on SEK 290 million of promotional sales | — |
| Verdict in V_PROMO_EFFECTIVENESS | Strong sales weak margin | — |

The campaign did what it was meant to do for traffic. Fresh & Produce sales at Kronmat grew about 12 percent over the rolling 12 months, well ahead of the banner, and transactions in the campaign weeks were up in every region. But the category's gross margin fell about 2 points over the same 12 months, and Sommarpris alone explains most of that.

## 3. Why the margin went

- Retailer funding at 30 percent depth with no supplier contribution. Comparable supplier-funded campaigns dilute margin by half as much.
- Waste did not fall. Volume rose but so did the short-dated share; markdown value in the campaign weeks was 40 percent above baseline.
- Halo was limited. Basket size on Sommarpris transactions was below the Kronmat average: customers bought the promoted items and little else.

## 4. Decisions

1. Sommarpris 2027 will be shared-funded with at most 20 percent depth or will not run.
2. Fresh & Produce margin is now tracked monthly in the banner P&L alongside sales growth; "growth with margin erosion" is a standing item in the Kronmat category review.
3. Campaign verdicts from V_PROMO_EFFECTIVENESS are reviewed quarterly across banners; 14 of 60 campaigns in the last 24 months are "Strong sales weak margin".
