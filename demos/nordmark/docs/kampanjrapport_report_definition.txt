[DESCRIPTION]
For each campaign week (vecka) and article (artikel) of a chain (kedja): planned quantity, the
sold forecast from the group's CatBoost demand model, the purchase forecast from the chain's own
plan (kedjans prognos), the actual quantity sold, and the forecast error of each forecast. Grouped by
campaign and tactic type. Used in the Monday planning review and in the post-campaign evaluation.

[NOTES - BUSINESS RULES]
1. Campaign week (Vecka) is the ISO week of the first delivery day, KAMPANJ.LEVERANS_FRAN. Selling days (KAMPANJ_START..KAMPANJ_SLUT)
   fall in the same or the following week; the report is keyed on the delivery week.
2. Forecast error is (forecast - actual sold) / actual sold. A positive value means over-forecast (more bought or predicted than sold).
3. Såld prognos is the group CatBoost demand model output (loaded by the catboost_forecast_feed task). Köpt prognos is the chain's own
   purchase plan (kedjans prognos) as entered in the chain planning tool; Planerad kvantitet is the purchase plan rounded up to whole
   cases (kolli) and is what was ordered.
4. Rows for future weeks (no actual sold yet) show M01..M03 only; M04..M06 are blank. They appear when P02 is set to a future week.
5. Quantities are consumer units (st, kg or l as per ARTIKEL.ENHET), never SEK, except M10.
6. Cost and margin are NOT in this report. The finance system (purchase cost, margin) has not been migrated to the warehouse;
   the planning team uses the illustrative extract ARTIKEL_KOSTNAD_MANAD (labelled KALLA = 'ILLUSTRATIV - finanssystem ej migrerat')
   for the margin effect of over-forecasting, outside this report.
7. Totals are ratios of sums. Do not average the row percentages; the analytical engine recomputes M05/M06 at every level.
8. Chain naming: Kedja = Kronmat, Torghallen, Grosshallen. The same report serves all three via the per-chain service accounts.
