Create a live app called "Store Performance Cockpit" for Nordmark Dagligvaror, a Swedish grocery group with three banners (Kronmat discount, Torghallen neighbourhood, Grosshallen hypermarkets acquired in January 2025). It is an operational cockpit for the Head of Store Operations, not a reporting dashboard. Every page answers "which stores are underperforming their local potential, why, and what should we test first". Use three connected pages.

DATA RULES
Use V_BANNER_KPI_MONTHLY for group and banner KPIs and V_KPI_STORE_MONTHLY for store KPIs by month. Use V_STORE_GAP_DECOMPOSITION, V_PEER_COMPARISON, V_CATCHMENT_POTENTIAL, V_AVAILABILITY_VS_SALES, V_LOYALTY_SEGMENT_TRENDS, V_PROMO_EFFECTIVENESS and COMPETITOR_EVENTS (joined to STORES) for drill-downs. Aggregate each source before combining; never join fact tables as raw rows. Anchor "today" to 2026-09-30. The latest complete month is September 2026 and the rolling 12 months are 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30. Like-for-like growth uses comparable stores only (IS_LFL_COMPARABLE); margins and rates are ratios of sums, never averages of store percentages. On-shelf availability target is 96 percent and the alert threshold is 94 percent. All amounts are SEK excluding VAT.

PAGE 1: EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
KPI tiles for the rolling 12 months: net sales, like-for-like growth, gross margin percent, transactions, average basket, on-shelf availability, with the change versus the prior 12 months. Filters for banner, region, catchment type and period. One chart that shows the core trade-off: like-for-like growth against gross margin percent for every banner and region, with Grosshallen highlighted. Breakdowns by banner (sales, LFL, margin, EBIT by quarter from V_BANNER_KPI_MONTHLY so the Grosshallen turn to break-even in 2026 is visible), by region and by category (last 13 weeks sales growth and availability). A ranked table of stores by potential gap in SEK from V_CATCHMENT_POTENTIAL with the potential index, cluster median index and potential band. Preselect Grosshallen Västerhamn so the demo starts there.

PAGE 2: STORE GAP LAB
For the selected store, show four panels. Gap decomposition: rolling-12-month sales change split into the transactions effect and the basket effect with the growth driver (V_STORE_GAP_DECOMPOSITION). Peer comparison: the store against its peer cluster median on sales per sqm, LFL growth, gross margin, availability, markdown rate, waste rate, private-label share and transactions per household (V_PEER_COMPARISON), with the gaps coloured. Local market: catchment potential, potential index, demographics, competitor count and the competitor events near the store with dates and distances (V_CATCHMENT_POTENTIAL and COMPETITOR_EVENTS). Causes: Dairy and Bread & Bakery availability over the last 13 weeks against the 94 percent threshold (V_AVAILABILITY_VS_SALES), loyalty segment visit changes with Families highlighted (V_LOYALTY_SEGMENT_TRENDS), and the store's monthly sales and transactions versus last year around the competitor opening date (V_KPI_STORE_MONTHLY). Then show three scenarios side by side: Baseline, Custom and System Recommended. Baseline is the current state. Custom has four controls: availability target (91 to 98 percent), private-label share target (14 to 24 percent), markdown rate reduction (0 to 50 percent) and Families visit recovery (0 to 25 percent). System Recommended copies the best store in the same peer cluster (Grosshallen Nordstaden) on each lever and ranks the candidate actions by estimated sales upside with a plain-language reason. Assumptions panel, editable and labelled as assumptions: each point of availability is worth 0.6 percent of sales, each point of private-label share is worth 0.3 percent of gross margin, halving the markdown rate is worth 0.5 points of margin, recovered Families visits carry the store average basket. Compare the scenarios on annual sales upside, gross profit upside, potential index after the change, cost of the test and confidence (High, Medium, Low). The user explicitly selects one scenario; the selection flows to the Implementation Plan.

PAGE 3: IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
Receive the selected scenario and prepopulate a 12-week test plan for the store phased by confidence: quick wins (availability order parameters and a second bread delivery in weeks 1 to 2), a controlled pilot (private-label relisting and targeted Families offers through the loyalty program in weeks 3 to 8) and levers to monitor and hold (markdown policy and non-food safety stock for late import suppliers). Include a leadership summary written from the current assumptions, weekly monitoring thresholds (transactions versus the peer cluster, availability above 95 percent by week 4, private-label share, markdown rate, Families visits), the concrete queue of actions with owners, due dates and success metrics, and a stop rule. Let the user edit any field and approve. On submit, save the plan to an action log inside the app and show a labelled mock payload for the store task-management handoff; state plainly that no external system is connected.

DESIGN
Calm, operational, few KPI tiles. Top stepper Executive Overview -> Store Gap Lab -> Implementation Plan. Preserve the selected store and scenario across pages. Tables where precision matters, tabular numerals for SEK amounts, percentages with one decimal.
