Create a live app called "Kampanjrapport" for Nordmark Dagligvaror, a Swedish grocery group with three chains (Kronmat discount, Torghallen neighbourhood, Grosshallen hypermarkets). It replaces the campaign planners' weekly MicroStrategy report "Kampanjrapport" (campaign performance and forecast accuracy by chain and week) and then goes beyond it: explain the variance, show what it costs, and draft the order adjustment for approval. The persona is the Head of Campaign Planning. Use three connected pages.

DATA RULES
Use V_KAMPANJRAPPORT as the only source for the report grid (one row per campaign week and article, key NYCKEL). Use V_PROGNOSFEL_TAKTIK for bias by tactic type and perishability, V_KAMPANJ_BUTIK_AVVIKELSE for the store drill, V_KAMPANJ_MARGINALEFFEKT for the margin cost and V_KAMPANJ_KOMMANDE for upcoming campaigns and the order adjustment draft. Aggregate each source before combining; never join fact tables as raw rows. Anchor "today" to 2026-09-30; completed campaign weeks run to 2026-W39 and weeks 2026-W41 to 2026-W44 are planned. Forecast error percent = (forecast minus actual sold) divided by actual sold, positive means over-forecast; totals are ratios of sums, never averages of row percentages. Såld prognos is the CatBoost demand model, Köpt prognos is the chain's own purchase plan. Cost data is illustrative because the finance system is not migrated: every cost figure must show the label from the KALLA column. Quantities are units; one decimal on percentages; Swedish column labels with the English term in parentheses on first use.

PAGE 1: RAPPORTEN
The familiar report, exactly as a MicroStrategy user knows it. Filters: Kedja (chain), Vecka (campaign week label), Kampanj, Taktiktyp. Preselect Kronmat and 2026-W36. A grid with the fixed column order: Vecka, Kampanj, Taktik, Nyckel, Artikel, Kategori, Färskvara, Planerad kvantitet, Såld prognos (CatBoost), Köpt prognos (kedjan), Faktisk såld, Prognosfel CatBoost %, Prognosfel kedjan %, sorted by Taktik, Kampanj, Artikel, with a totals row computed as ratios of sums. Drill path Kedja to Kampanj to Taktik to Artikel. Four small tiles above the grid for the selected chain and week: articles, actual sold, chain forecast error %, CatBoost forecast error %. Under the grid a compact bias-by-tactic table for the same chain and week from V_PROGNOSFEL_TAKTIK. The user picks a tactic type or campaign row; the selection flows to page 2.

PAGE 2: FÖRKLARA OCH KOSTNAD
For the selected chain, week and tactic (default Kronmat, 2026-W36, Multiköp 3 för 2): a variance decomposition in this fixed order. Bias by tactic type over the last 13 completed campaign weeks (2026-W27 to 2026-W39), then perishable versus dry goods with the share of absolute error, then the top 10 over-forecast articles, then the store drill from V_KAMPANJ_BUTIK_AVVIKELSE: stores of the chain ranked by deviation over the rolling 12 months with the peer cluster median beside each and the competitor-event flag (HAR_KONKURRENTHANDELSE_90D) marked; for Grosshallen the panel must make Grosshallen Västerhamn's deviation before and after 2026-03-16 visible. Then the cost panel from V_KAMPANJ_MARGINALEFFEKT: quarterly margin cost of over-forecasting by chain and tactic, split into waste on perishables and clearance markdowns, with Q3 2026 highlighted and the KALLA label printed in the panel title ("illustrative cost data, finance system not migrated"). A short plain-language explanation panel that states the bias, the top drivers with their share, and uses the words "coincides with", never "caused by".

PAGE 3: ORDERJUSTERING
Upcoming campaign weeks from V_KAMPANJ_KOMMANDE: a table of planned campaigns for 2026-W41 to 2026-W44 with chain, tactic, delivery date, days to delivery, chain forecast, CatBoost forecast, gap %, flagged articles and margin protected; flagged perishable campaigns (gap above 15 percent) highlighted. Preselect Äpplen & Päron (Torghallen, 2026-W42). For the selected campaign an order adjustment draft, same layout every time: header with campaign, chain, week, delivery date and days to delivery; one row per article with Artikel, Kategori, Kolli, Köpt prognos, Såld prognos CatBoost, Gap %, Föreslagen minskning (kolli, whole cases rounded down to full layers), units cut, Skyddad marginal SEK; totals and the cut as a percentage of the chain forecast; a "Monitor" list of articles under the threshold; an editable threshold control (10 to 25 percent, default 15) that recomputes the flags and the rounding; a planner summary of at most five sentences; and an approval block with Requested by, Data used (the views and the as-of date), Proposed by, Approver (the campaign's ansvarig planerare from KAMPANJ) and Status DRAFT. An Approve button that records who approved and when in an action log inside the app and shows a labelled mock hand-off payload for the order system; state plainly that no external system is connected and nothing is sent.

DESIGN
Calm, dense, report-like. Top stepper Rapporten -> Förklara och kostnad -> Orderjustering. Preserve the selected chain, week, tactic and campaign across pages. Tabular numerals, thousands separators, percentages with one decimal. Every cost figure carries the illustrative label.
