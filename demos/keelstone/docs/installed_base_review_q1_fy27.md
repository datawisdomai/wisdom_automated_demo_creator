Letterhead: Keelstone Data Systems — Customer Success and Sales Operations

To: Executive staff
From: Victoria Lang (RVP Americas East), Ravi Kaur (VP Customer Success), Grace Osei (VP Sales Operations)
Subject: Q1 FY27 installed base review and Americas East renewal exposure

## Summary

- Finance ARR closed Q1 FY27 (July) at about 486 million dollars, up 4 percent on the April close. Growth came from cloud commits and KS-9000 attach; support ARR on the KS-7000 family is shrinking as expected.
- Americas East renewal rate for Q1 FY27 fell to about 79 percent of ARR due against 90 percent for FY26. The other three regions held within three points of last year.
- Two causes, both visible in the data: the KS-7000 end-of-support announcement (bulletin PLB-2026-014, 15 June) and the KeelOS 9.14.2 controller failover defect that hit Americas East customers first because that region received the managed upgrade in July and August.
- Most of the shortfall is pending rather than lost. Customers are waiting for a migration plan and for the 9.14.3 fix before they commit to another support term.
- Actions: a KS-7000 migration offer for every Americas East account with a renewal inside 120 days, a pause on managed upgrades until 9.14.3 is qualified, and a weekly at-risk review run from the certified renewals view.

## KeelOS 9.14.2

- KeelOS 9.14.2 shipped 1 July. The AutoPulse managed upgrade campaign ran in Americas East in July and August and was scheduled for the other regions from September.
- Defect KB-2026-0917 causes a controller failover loop during the post-upgrade giveback on KS-7000 and KS-9000 controllers. It produced production outages at customers that had upgraded.
- Since July, Americas East has logged 66 P1 cases on 9.14.2 against four or fewer in any other region. The engineering fix, 9.14.3, shipped 15 September and is being rolled out with customer approval only.
- Customer sentiment in the case notes is poor. Several customers have linked the outage to their January support renewal and asked for a written root cause analysis and migration pricing before they sign.

## Renewal exposure

- Americas East has about 28 million dollars of ARR renewing in the next 90 days, of which the renewals team has flagged roughly 13 million at risk. The two reasons that dominate are "End of support product" and "Open P1 case".
- The pending backlog in Americas East is about 10 million dollars across roughly 110 lapsed contracts whose opportunities are still open. The Sales Ops ARR extract still counts these, which is why its figure is about 7 percent above Finance ARR for the region.
- Altura Technology Partners, the region's largest partner, has attached support to only about 61 percent of the systems it installed this fiscal year against 77 percent last year. Unattached systems become unprotected renewals twelve months later.

## Cloud

- Keelstone Cloud Volumes consumption for June to September is tracking about 78 percent above the same period last year.
- 27 enterprise accounts are consuming less than 60 percent of their FY27 commit (about 3.6 million dollars unconsumed). These accounts should be right-sized or expanded at renewal rather than allowed to lapse.
