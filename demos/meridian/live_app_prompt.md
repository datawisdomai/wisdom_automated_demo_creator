Create a live app called "Contract Reconciliation Review" for Meridian Clinician Network, a professional network for US clinicians whose B2B agreements with pharma brands and health systems must agree across DocuSign (the signed contract), Salesforce (the closed-won opportunity) and NetSuite (the sales order and billing). It is a review workflow for the Chief Accounting Officer's teams (Sales Ops, Revenue Accounting, Billing), not a reporting dashboard. Every page answers "which deals do not reconcile, why, who owns the fix, and what did we decide". Use three connected pages.

DATA RULES
Today is 2026-09-30 (Q3 2026 quarter end). There is no precomputed results table: the findings come from running the eleven certified three-way checks live against the raw tables and the helper views. Use the certified queries from the query library for each check (TCV_MISMATCH, PRODUCT_LINE_COUNT_MISMATCH, OPPORTUNITY_WITHOUT_CONTRACT_LINK, AMENDMENT_NOT_IN_SALESFORCE, TERM_END_DATE_MISMATCH, AMENDMENT_NOT_IN_NETSUITE, DUPLICATE_SALES_ORDERS, SALES_ORDER_WITHOUT_OPPORTUNITY, PAYMENT_TERMS_MISMATCH, BILLING_FREQUENCY_MISMATCH, MISSING_BILLING_MILESTONE), or the single certified query "Run the full three-way reconciliation and list every finding ranked by exposure." Every finding row carries chain id, contract number, customer, customer type, account owner, check type, field name, contract value, Salesforce value, NetSuite value, variance in USD, severity, owning team, SOX control reference, corrective system and a plain-language reason. Join RECONCILIATION_CONTROLS on check type for the check definition and tolerance. Use V_CHAIN_SYSTEM_SUMMARY for the three systems side by side per agreement, V_AMENDMENT_LINEAGE for the chain of amendments, V_DEAL_CROSSWALK for the opportunity and sales order ids, and the raw CONTRACTS, CONTRACT_LINE_ITEMS, CONTRACT_BILLING_SCHEDULE, SALESFORCE_OPPORTUNITIES, SALESFORCE_OPPORTUNITY_PRODUCTS, NETSUITE_SALES_ORDERS, NETSUITE_SALES_ORDER_LINES and NETSUITE_BILLING_SCHEDULE tables for evidence. Exposure is the sum of the absolute variance across findings. Never sum CONTRACTS.TOTAL_CONTRACT_VALUE across versions of one agreement; the latest document carries the current value.

PAGE 1: FINDINGS QUEUE
A summary strip at the top: total findings, agreements with findings out of agreements checked (mismatch rate), total exposure in USD, High severity count, and the split of findings and exposure by owning team (Sales Ops, Revenue Accounting, Billing).
Below it one table with every finding, one row per finding, columns: customer, contract number, check type, field, contract value, Salesforce value, NetSuite value, variance USD, severity, owning team, SOX control, reason. Sort by exposure descending by default. Filters: owning team, severity, customer type, account owner, check type, and a free-text search on customer. Show a small count of findings per check type that acts as a filter. Clicking a row opens that finding on the Case Review page. Preselect the Halvorsen Therapeutics findings so the demo starts there.

PAGE 2: CASE REVIEW
For the selected finding show the evidence side by side in three columns: Contract (the latest signed document's terms: contract number, signed date, TCV, effective and end dates, payment terms, billing frequency, products from CONTRACT_LINE_ITEMS and the billing milestones from CONTRACT_BILLING_SCHEDULE, plus the amendment chain from V_AMENDMENT_LINEAGE with each version's date, TCV change and what changed), Salesforce (the chain's opportunities with amount, close date, linked contract number, end date and products), NetSuite (the chain's sales orders with order total, service dates, payment terms, billing schedule type, last Boomi sync and the billing schedule lines). Highlight the field the finding is about in every column where it appears, and show the check definition, tolerance and SOX control from RECONCILIATION_CONTROLS in a side panel with the plain-language reason.
Below the evidence a decision form: Decision (Confirmed, Rejected as false positive, Needs follow-up), Target system to correct (Salesforce, NetSuite, Contract re-review), a free-text note, and a Save button. Saved decisions persist within the app session and show as a badge on the finding in the queue. Provide previous and next buttons to move through the filtered queue. Also allow the reviewer to pick any agreement from a dropdown to see all of its findings together.

PAGE 3: EXPORT
Show the reviewed findings (those with a decision, and optionally all findings) as a JSON array: one object per finding with every field above plus decision, reviewer_note, target_system, reviewed_at and a run_id built from the as-of date. Display it in a code block with a Copy button and counts of confirmed, rejected and follow-up decisions by owning team. State plainly on the page that this is what would be written back to Snowflake, NetSuite or a ticketing system and that the write is a one-line integration away; nothing is written by this app.

DESIGN
Calm, operational, tables where precision matters, tabular numerals for money, severity as a coloured badge. Top stepper Findings Queue -> Case Review -> Export. Preserve filters, the selected finding and the saved decisions across pages. Keep the SOX control reference visible on every finding.
