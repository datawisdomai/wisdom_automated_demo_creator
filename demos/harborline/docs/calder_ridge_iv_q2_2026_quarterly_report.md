## 2. Capital activity during the quarter

- Capital call notice 2026-14 on 14 April 2026 for USD 40.0 million, applied to the follow-on investment in Meridian Dental Partners and to management fees.
- Capital call notice 2026-15 on 2 June 2026 for USD 24.0 million, applied to the add-on acquisition by Tollgate Industrial Services and to partial repayment of the subscription facility.
- Distribution 2026-D3 on 20 May 2026 of USD 96.0 million (realization proceeds from the sale of Northgate Analytics to a strategic acquirer). The distribution is not recallable.
- Total called in the quarter: USD 64.0 million. Total distributed in the quarter: USD 96.0 million. Net cash flow to limited partners: USD +32.0 million.

## 3. Subscription credit facility

The fund maintains a USD 400.0 million subscription credit facility secured on undrawn commitments. The balance drawn was USD 210.0 million at 30 June 2026 (USD 232.0 million at 31 March 2026), equal to 8.0 percent of NAV. The facility was used during the quarter to bridge the Tollgate add-on acquisition ahead of call notice 2026-15. The General Partner reports IRR both with and without the facility in the appendix; the facility adds approximately 0.6 percentage points to the net IRR since inception, within the 1.0 point disclosure threshold agreed with the Limited Partner Advisory Committee.

## 4. Portfolio commentary

Fund IV holds 14 portfolio companies. Northgate Analytics was fully realized in May 2026 at 3.1x invested capital. Meridian Dental Partners and Tollgate Industrial Services received follow-on capital. Two companies (Ironbark Logistics and Fairhaven Specialty Foods) were marked down modestly on lower trailing multiples; the remaining positions were marked up in line with public comparables. The General Partner expects two further exits in the next four quarters subject to market conditions.

## 5. Cash-flow pacing outlook

Remaining uncalled commitments are USD 320.0 million (10.0 percent), reserved for follow-ons and fees. The General Partner expects to call USD 60 to 90 million over the next four quarters and to distribute USD 250 to 400 million, which would take DPI to between 0.49x and 0.54x by 30 June 2027. These are estimates and not commitments.
