# Demo Plan: Harborline Private Capital

**Prospect / vertical:** State Street Data Intelligence (Financial Services, private-capital data and index provider) · **Persona:** Nan Zhang, Head of Product Development (the demo is shown *to* him; the Live App is built *for his allocator clients*) · **As-of date:** 2026-10-15; latest reported quarter is Q2 2026 (2026-06-30, the normal one-quarter private-capital lag)

## Context
Nan is building an AI layer that turns a client's plain-English request into a configured custom index or dashboard on his portal, and has not settled on a production model. The AE wants a 30-minute demo whose centerpiece is an allocator prompt producing a finished interactive app in seconds, refined conversationally, plus a synthetic private-capital dataset (cash flows, NAVs, vintages, strategies, regions, a line-of-credit field) and one unstructured fund quarterly report so extraction is part of the story. The build priority is simplicity and speed. Nothing in the demo names State Street.

## The story in one paragraph
Harborline Private Capital is a fictional producer of private-markets benchmarks and indices (a "quant shop that does not trade"): it ingests fund cash flows and NAVs from contributing LPs and GPs, extracts figures from fund quarterly reports, and publishes the Harborline Private Capital Index family. Its allocator clients (pensions, endowments) receive the indices and today configure custom indices and pacing views by hand on a portal. The demo shows an allocator asking for a custom index in plain English and getting a working app in seconds, refining it conversationally, asking a multi-step portfolio-versus-benchmark question in chat, reading a fund's quarterly report PDF next to the structured NAV, and an agent that watches subscription-line distortion of IRR.

## Data (Snowflake `HARBORLINE_POC.ANALYTICS`, data from 2012-01-01)
- Dimensions (4): `MANAGERS` (~110 GPs), `FUNDS` (~380: strategy Buyout/Growth/Venture/Private Credit/Real Estate/Infrastructure; region North America/Europe/Asia-Pacific/Global; vintage 2012–2024; fund size and size bucket; **`USES_SUBSCRIPTION_LINE` and `SUBSCRIPTION_LINE_LIMIT_USD_M`**), `BENCHMARKS` (~12 Harborline indices: broad PE plus strategy × region), `ALLOCATORS` (5 clients).
- Facts (5): `FUND_CASH_FLOWS` (~25k capital calls and distributions by date), `FUND_QUARTERLY_NAV` (~12k fund-quarters: NAV, cumulative called/distributed, **`CREDIT_LINE_BALANCE_USD_M`**, since-inception IRR, TVPI, DPI, RVPI), `BENCHMARK_QUARTERLY` (~700 rows: pooled IRR, quarterly return, TVPI, DPI), `ALLOCATOR_COMMITMENTS` (~160), `FUND_REPORT_EXTRACTS` (~760: figures extracted from the last two quarterly reports per fund with a confidence score; the hero fund's row matches its PDF).
- Views (7): `V_FUND_PERFORMANCE_LATEST`, `V_FUND_PERFORMANCE_QUARTERLY`, `V_COHORT_INDEX_QUARTERLY` (strategy × region × vintage × size bucket × quarter: fund count, median IRR, pooled TVPI/DPI, i.e. the custom-index engine), `V_BENCHMARK_QUARTERLY`, `V_CASH_FLOW_PACING_QUARTERLY`, `V_SUBSCRIPTION_LINE_IRR_GAP`, `V_ALLOCATOR_VINTAGE_VS_BENCHMARK`; plus `DATA_DICTIONARY`.
- IRR is pre-computed per fund-quarter by the generator (what a data vendor publishes), so no XIRR in SQL.
- Documents (1 PDF, attached to the domain for chat): *Calder Ridge Capital Partners IV — Q2 2026 Quarterly Report to Limited Partners* (NAV, calls, distributions, subscription-line balance, portfolio commentary, pacing outlook).
- Context assets (through the context extractor only, never CSV; ≤ 3 files per run, triggered concurrently; small files): Harborline Index Methodology and Metric Definitions standard, Business Glossary and Data Ownership Register, Data Platform wiki page for `HARBORLINE_POC`, #index-desk chat export, plus the quarterly report above. The pipeline adds the dbt semantic-layer YAML, a small certified query library and an analyst handbook rendered from the local metrics / reviewed queries / knowledge.
- Final step: Domain Health analysis queued and left running for the SE.

## What the audience sees
1. **Live App "Custom Index Studio"** built from the allocator prompt *"Build me a custom index of North American buyout funds, vintages 2015–2020, and compare quarterly IRR to the broad PE benchmark"*, with the two refinements as its pages: Index Builder (cohort vs broad index, quarterly IRR, fund count, TVPI/DPI) → Split by Fund Size → Cash-Flow Pacing (calls, distributions, net cash flow and DPI by age for the cohort against older vintages). The SE builds it live from the prompt in the demo; the pre-built app is the safety net and the script says so.
2. **Follow-up question** (chat): "Compare Westbrook State Retirement System's commitments by vintage year to the matching Harborline benchmark and tell me which vintage lags the most and which funds explain the gap" → expected: 2018 vintage lags by ~2+ pts; the two venture funds named with their IRRs; other vintages at or above benchmark; commitment-weighted IRR per vintage. A second scripted chat beat reads the PDF: "What does the Calder Ridge IV Q2 2026 report say about its subscription line and does it match the NAV we hold".
3. **Agent "Subscription Line Watch"** (created disabled): each quarter lists funds whose credit-line balance exceeds 15% of NAV or whose IRR sits more than 3 pts above line-free peers in the same vintage and strategy, and posts the summary.
4. **30-minute script** in the Shipping Cockpit format ordered the way the AE asked: (1) NL → configured portal view, ~10 min; (2) governed answers and the extraction/PDF beat; (3) the "why not ChatGPT" objection, answered with certified metrics, chain of evidence, eval scores and the Domain Health suggestions.
